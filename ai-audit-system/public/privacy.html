Privacy policy

How we handle your information.

Plain English, no fine print. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Who we are

Volve Solutions is a sole trader business run by Hugh Douglas in Picton NSW, Australia. Our ABN is 60 928 990 855. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Volve Solutions. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Call recordings and transcripts. Calls to our free audit line (02 5700 0037) and to Volve Righto AI receptionists are recorded and transcribed. Callers are told at the start of the call.
  • Details you give us on a call. For example your name, business name, phone number, email address, suburb, and information about how your business runs.
  • Details your customers give on a receptionist call. For example a caller's name, number, suburb and the job they need.
  • Details you send through our forms. Our trial and enquiry forms open a prepared email, so we receive what you choose to send.
  • Website audit inputs. The website address, business name, industry and any Google Business Profile details you enter. We read only the public web page you submit.

Why we use it

We use this information to run your audit and prepare your report, to run your receptionist and send you caller details, to reply to your enquiries, and to improve our service. We do not sell personal information. We only contact you about the things you asked us about.

Who processes it, and where

We use a small number of service providers to run Volve. Some store or process data overseas, mainly in the United States:

  • Retell AI runs our AI phone assistants, call recording and transcription (United States).
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that helps analyse audit calls before Hugh reviews them (United States).
  • Resend sends report and notification emails (United States).
  • Railway hosts this website and our application (United States).
  • Our phone and SMS carriers connect calls and deliver text messages.

We choose providers that protect data with reasonable security measures. By using our services you understand your information may be handled overseas by these providers.

How long we keep it

We keep recordings, transcripts and details only as long as we need them to deliver the service you asked for, answer questions about it, and meet any legal obligations. After that we delete them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, including a call recording. Email hugh@volvesolutions.com.au and Hugh will respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days.

Complaints

If you are worried about how we have handled your information, email hugh@volvesolutions.com.au first and we will try to sort it out. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
