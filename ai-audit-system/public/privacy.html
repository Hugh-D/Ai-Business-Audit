Privacy and call recording

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Plain English, because you should not need a lawyer to work out what happens to a phone call. If anything here is unclear, call us and ask.

Last updated 3 September 2026

Who we are

Volve Solutions, based in Sydney, Australia. We provide a free AI business audit by phone and build custom automations for Australian service businesses.

Contact us about anything on this page at volvesolutions@outlook.com or on 1300 AI GROW (1300 244 769).

The audit call

When you call the audit line you speak to an automated voice assistant. Before any recording starts, the assistant tells you the call will be recorded and asks your permission.

If you say no, the call ends there and nothing is recorded. Consent is the whole basis for this. We do not record without it.

If you consent, we collect:

  • An audio recording of the call
  • A written transcript of that recording
  • Your business name, contact details and the operational details you describe
  • The phone number you called from

What we do with it

We use the recording and transcript for one purpose: preparing your audit report. The process is:

  1. The transcript is cleaned up and structured.
  2. It is analysed to identify where revenue and time are being lost.
  3. Hugh reads every finding and edits the report before it is sent.
  4. The finished report is emailed to you.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not add you to a marketing list because you called.

Who else touches your data

We use third-party services to run the audit. They process your information on our instructions and only for these purposes:

  • Voice platform. Handles the phone call, the recording and the transcription.
  • AI analysis provider. Processes the transcript to draft the audit findings. Your transcript is not used to train their models.
  • Hosting and email providers. Store the call record and deliver your report.

Some of these providers operate servers outside Australia, which means your information may be processed overseas.

How long we keep it

We keep your recording, transcript and report for as long as we need them to prepare your report, answer your questions about it and meet our record-keeping obligations.

You can ask us to delete your recording and transcript at any time. Email us and we will confirm once it is done.

The website audit tool

If you use the free website audit on our home page, we read the public web page you submit. Nothing else. There is no login, we do not access anything private, and we do not store the results against you.

Any Google Business Profile details you enter are information you type in yourself, taken from a publicly visible profile. We use them to calculate the score shown on screen.

We record the IP address of requests briefly, only to stop the tool being abused.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your recording, transcript and report
  • Complain if you think we have mishandled your information

Email volvesolutions@outlook.com and we will respond within 30 days. If we cannot sort it out between us, you can take a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Still have a question about your data?

Call and ask before you book the audit, not after.
