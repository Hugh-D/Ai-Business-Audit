# Electrician Prospecting SOP

## Objective

Build a list of 20 to 30 electrician prospects, then convert at least 10 into completed free audit calls.

Do not research forever. The list only needs to be good enough to start conversations.

## Target Geography

Start local or near-local unless there is a reason not to.

Default first pass:

- Electricians within easy timezone reach.
- Owner-operated or manager-led electrical businesses.
- Residential service, small commercial service, or mixed service/install.
- Visible Google profile, website, and phone number.

## Prospect Sources

Use these in order:

1. Google Maps: search `electrician near me`, `emergency electrician`, `residential electrician`, `commercial electrician`.
2. Local Facebook business/community groups.
3. LinkedIn search for owners, directors, or operations managers at electrical contractors.
4. Existing network referrals.
5. Electrical trade association directories.

## Qualification Rules

Add a prospect when at least 4 are true:

- They have 3 to 25 visible staff or look owner-led.
- They advertise phone-led service calls or urgent work.
- Their website has a clear phone number.
- They have Google reviews.
- They mention residential, small commercial, maintenance, safety checks, smoke alarms, switchboards, lighting, EV chargers, or emergency callouts.
- Their website/contact flow looks like it may leak leads.
- Their reviews are good enough that they likely care about growth.

Skip for now when:

- They are a large tender/commercial-only contractor.
- They look like a solo side business with no admin capacity.
- They have no reachable decision-maker path.
- Their service area is unclear.
- They are not likely to value follow-up, reviews, or booking improvements.

## What To Record

Use [electrician-audit-tracker.csv](electrician-audit-tracker.csv).

Minimum useful fields:

- Business
- Contact
- Phone / Email
- Source
- Main leak hypothesis
- Next step
- Notes

Do not wait to find perfect contact data. If the phone number is public and the business fits, add it.

## First Pass Workflow

For each prospect:

1. Open the website and Google profile.
2. Check whether they handle calls, quotes, urgent jobs, or maintenance.
3. Look for one likely leak: missed calls, quote follow-up, reviews, booking/admin, website conversion, repeat work, software workflow.
4. Add the prospect to the tracker.
5. Write the next action: call, DM, email, referral ask, or local group reply.

Timebox:

- 60 minutes to collect the first 20 prospects.
- 90 minutes maximum before outreach starts.

## Outreach Cadence

Day 1:

- Contact 10 prospects.
- Use the direct message or phone opener from [ELECTRICIAN_LAUNCH_PACK.md](ELECTRICIAN_LAUNCH_PACK.md).
- Mark `Outcome` after each contact.

Day 2:

- Contact the next 10 prospects.
- Follow up with anyone from Day 1 who showed interest but did not book.

Day 3:

- Fill gaps until 30 prospects are listed.
- Push for booked audit calls, not long explanations.

## Daily Scoreboard

Track these numbers at the end of each day:

- New prospects added.
- Prospects contacted.
- Replies/conversations.
- Audit calls booked.
- Audit calls completed.
- Reports sent.
- Follow-up calls booked.
- Sprint opportunities.

## Booking Ask

Use this once a prospect shows interest:

> Great. The audit takes about 15 to 20 minutes by phone. It asks about calls, quotes, follow-up, reviews, and booking workflow, then I review the report before sending it over. Would today or tomorrow suit better?

Once the audit number is ready, replace the booking ask with:

> Great. The easiest way is to call the audit number when you have 15 to 20 minutes. I will review the report afterwards and send it through.

## Stop Rules

Stop adding prospects and change the message if:

- 20 contacted prospects produce zero replies.
- 10 conversations produce zero audit bookings.
- Prospects understand the offer but do not care.

Do not respond by adding app features. First change the niche angle, pain wording, or outreach channel.
