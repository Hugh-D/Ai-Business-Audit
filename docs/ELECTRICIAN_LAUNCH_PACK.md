# Electrician Launch Pack

## Launch Decision

First niche: electricians and small electrical contractors.

Keep the offer narrow:

> Find where electrical businesses are losing booked jobs, quote follow-up, reviews, and repeat work through a quick AI-led business audit.

The goal is not to sell "AI". The goal is to use AI to diagnose revenue leakage, then sell practical implementation work.

## Best First Prospects

Use [ELECTRICIAN_PROSPECTING_SOP.md](ELECTRICIAN_PROSPECTING_SOP.md) to build the first 20 to 30 prospect list.

Prioritise owner-operated or manager-led electrical businesses with:

- 3 to 25 staff.
- Residential service, small commercial service, or mixed service/install work.
- Google reviews and visible local search presence.
- A phone number on the website.
- Signs they handle urgent calls, quotes, bookings, or repeat maintenance.
- Weak follow-up signals: old reviews, vague website CTA, generic contact forms, no booking link, no clear after-hours process.

Avoid for the first 10:

- Very large contractors with procurement-heavy buying.
- Sole traders with no admin capacity and no obvious growth intent.
- Businesses that only do major commercial projects with long tender cycles.
- Anyone who needs a custom integration before they will take the audit seriously.

## Pain Hypotheses

Use the audits to test these, not to assume they are true:

- Missed calls after hours are not followed up quickly.
- Quote follow-up is inconsistent after site visits or inspection work.
- Admin staff know the process, but it is not documented.
- Reviews are requested manually, if at all.
- Website traffic leaks because the call-to-action is weak or trust signals are thin.
- Jobs are completed without a next-step prompt: switchboard upgrades, safety checks, maintenance reminders, smoke alarm compliance, EV charger interest, or future work.
- Software exists, but the workflow across phone, calendar, quotes, invoicing, and reviews is messy.

> Free Electrician Revenue Leak Audit

- A reviewed report showing their top 3 revenue leaks.
- A practical action plan for call handling, quote follow-up, reviews, and customer experience.
- Optional follow-up call with Hugh to discuss the findings.

Positioning line:

## Objection Handling

"Is this just an AI sales pitch?"

No. The audit uses AI for the phone assessment and report draft, but the output is about practical business workflow: missed calls, quote follow-up, reviews, booking, and admin. Hugh reviews the report before sending it.

"We already use software."

That is useful. The audit looks at whether the current tools are actually helping the workflow, especially around calls, quotes, follow-up, and reviews.

"We are too busy."

That is exactly why the audit is short. It is designed to find the one or two workflow leaks most likely to cost booked jobs or admin time.

"How much does it cost?"

The first 10 are free while the process is being validated. Later it will likely become a paid audit.

"What happens after?"

You receive the report. If there is a useful fix, Hugh can walk you through it. If not, you still have the findings.

## First Implementation Sprint Offer

Sell only after a completed audit reveals a real problem.

### Electrician Follow-Up Sprint

Promise:

> Stop warm electrical leads and quotes from going cold.

Scope:

- Map current call, quote, and follow-up workflow.
- Set up missed-call or uncontacted-lead text/email follow-up.
- Set up quote follow-up reminders or simple automation.
- Add a review request workflow for completed jobs.
- Provide a one-page operating checklist for admin or owner use.

Suggested price:

- Validation price: AUD $1,500 to $2,500.
- Standard price later: AUD $2,500 to $5,000.

Success metric:

- Faster response to missed calls or web leads.
- More quote follow-up attempts completed.
- More review requests sent after completed jobs.
- Fewer leads handled only from memory.

Do not sell:

- Full CRM rebuild.
- Custom AI receptionist.
- Multi-location reporting.
- Complex integrations.
- Ongoing retainer before one sprint has produced proof.

## Success Criteria

The electrician wedge is worth continuing if at least one of these is true after 10 audits:

- 3 or more follow-up calls are booked.
- 2 or more prospects ask about implementation help.
- 1 prospect buys or strongly commits to a sprint.
- The same leak appears in at least 5 audits.

If none of those happen, do not add product features. Change the niche, offer, or outreach angle first.
