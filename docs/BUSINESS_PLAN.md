# Business Plan

## Positioning

AI Business Audit helps service businesses find where revenue leaks out of their current process and shows which AI or automation changes are worth implementing first.

The strongest initial wedge is:

> A fast AI-readiness and revenue-leak audit for trades and home service companies.

The first validation niche is electricians and small electrical contractors. Keep the next 10 audits focused there before expanding back to other trades.

## Ideal First Customer

- Owner-operated or manager-led home service business.
- 5 to 50 employees.
- Gets inbound calls or web leads.
- Has inconsistent follow-up.
- Wants growth but does not know which AI tools are useful.
- Already pays for software, ads, answering services, or CRM tools.

## Offer Ladder

### Entry Offer: AI Business Audit

- Customer calls an advertised audit number and completes an AI-led assessment.
- AI/process audit report.
- Top 3 recommended fixes.
- Optional booked follow-up call with Hugh to discuss the report.
- Suggested price: free for validation, then $97 to $297.

### Core Offer: Implementation Sprint

- Set up one or two high-impact automations.
- Examples: missed-call text-back, quote follow-up, review request flow, lead triage, CRM cleanup.
- Suggested price: $1,500 to $5,000.

### Retainer: Automation Growth Partner

- Monthly optimization and support.
- Reporting, workflow maintenance, new automations.
- Suggested price: $750 to $2,500/month depending on complexity.

## Validation Plan

1. Start with electricians and small electrical contractors.
2. Run 10 opt-in inbound audits using the app and the public audit number.
3. Track the most common pain points.
4. Turn repeated recommendations into implementation packages.
5. Collect before/after metrics and testimonials.

## Sales Motion

- Outreach target: owner, operations manager, or office manager.
- Hook: "I help home service businesses find missed revenue in calls, follow-up, reviews, and booking workflows."
- CTA: "Call for a quick AI/process audit and see your top 3 leaks."
- Delivery: inbound AI assessment, generated report, optional follow-up call with Hugh.

## Metrics To Track

- Audit calls booked.
- Audits completed.
- Reports delivered.
- Implementation calls booked.
- Paid conversions.
- Average implementation value.
- Common recommended automations.
- Time from call to report.

## 30-Day Plan

Week 1: Make the app usable internally and run sample audits.

Week 2: Talk to 10 business owners and refine the report.

Week 3: Package the top 2 implementation offers.

Week 4: Sell the first paid implementation sprint.
